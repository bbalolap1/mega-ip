# Ordos

Ordos is the central character of the Version 0.0 prototype.

He originates from Earth, dies, reincarnates, and is born as an Origin God within Nana. His current domain is Order, and his early defining question is what a true god should become.

## Canon Boundary

The details above are taken from the foundation. Any final transformation beyond the search for true eternity remains unresolved.
