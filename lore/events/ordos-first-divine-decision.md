# Ordos' First Divine Decision

Ordos exists within Nana and recognizes his divine shadow. The shadow communicates a philosophy of corruption as natural. Ordos compares that idea with his memories of Earth and rejects the claim that good and evil must be balanced.

He removes the shadow. His divine nature changes. Nana reacts, other Origin Gods observe the change, and the first philosophical conflict begins.
